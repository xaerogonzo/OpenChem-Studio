### Phase B: an evidence hunt on PETN and the nitrate esters (benchmark records, nothing scored)
- `benchmarks/energetics_props/PETN_EVIDENCE.md` and `PETN_EVIDENCE.json` inventory what exists for PETN and its family against the preregistration's R1 and R2 conditions: PETN has no measured gas-phase enthalpy (its gas value is a solid value plus a sublimation, about -388 kJ/mol), pentaerythritol is the one four-arm compound with measured data and does not meet R1 as drafted, and the R1 structure pattern is also matched by a hydrocarbon. A COD pass outside the census window (`cod_nitrate_ester_pass.py`, `cod_nitrate_extra.json`) finds 73 nitrate-ester entries, 41 of them the census could not see. No number of the preregistration changed and no method was scored.
