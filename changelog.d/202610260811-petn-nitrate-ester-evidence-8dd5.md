### Phase B: an evidence hunt on PETN and the nitrate esters (benchmark records, nothing scored)
- `benchmarks/energetics_props/PETN_EVIDENCE.md` and `PETN_EVIDENCE.json` (87 rows) inventory what exists for PETN and its family against the preregistration's R1 and R2 conditions, with seven primary papers read on the page: PETN has no measured gas-phase enthalpy (derived -388 kJ/mol, W1-F12 -395.5), pentaerythritol is the one four-arm compound with measured data and does not meet R1 as drafted, the R1 pattern is also matched by a hydrocarbon, four printed enthalpies are one Cheetah input table and one of them (-371) is contradicted by 171 kJ/mol. A COD pass outside the census window (`cod_nitrate_ester_pass.py`, `cod_nitrate_extra.json`) finds 73 nitrate-ester entries, 41 of them the census could not see. The fit tables of the held papers were mined (they repeat NIST, so NIST holds at least 26 nitrate esters, the closest measured neighbours of PETN being solid-phase only; Cawkwell 2021 adds gas-phase nitroglycerin). Klapoetke's 4th edition, Viswanath, Akhavan's nitroglycerin table and Boddu were read too and add no independent value. Chavez 2008 and Barone 1990 were read, and the nitramine stratum (R2) was counted from readable sources: at least 19 names against about 150 needed. Values are quoted as facts with locators. No number of the preregistration changed and no method was scored.
