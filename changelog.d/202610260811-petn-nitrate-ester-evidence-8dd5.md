### Phase B: an evidence hunt on PETN and the nitrate esters (benchmark records, nothing scored)
- `benchmarks/energetics_props/PETN_EVIDENCE.md` and `PETN_EVIDENCE.json` (107 rows) inventory what exists for PETN and its family against the preregistration's R1 and R2 conditions, with seven primary papers read on the page: PETN has no measured gas-phase enthalpy (derived -388 kJ/mol, W1-F12 -395.5), pentaerythritol is the one four-arm compound with measured data and does not meet R1 as drafted, the R1 pattern is also matched by a hydrocarbon, four printed enthalpies are one Cheetah input table and one of them (-371) is contradicted by 171 kJ/mol. A COD pass outside the census window (`cod_nitrate_ester_pass.py`, `cod_nitrate_extra.json`) finds 73 nitrate-ester entries, 41 of them the census could not see. The fit tables of the held papers were mined (they repeat NIST, so NIST holds at least 26 nitrate esters, the closest measured neighbours of PETN being solid-phase only; Cawkwell 2021 adds gas-phase nitroglycerin). Klapoetke's 4th edition, Viswanath, Akhavan's nitroglycerin table and Boddu were read too and add no independent value. Chavez 2008 and Barone 1990 were read, and the nitramine stratum (R2) was counted: 14 nitramines in NIST (12 solid, 2 verified by the preregistration's agreement rule, 8 eligible for a holdout) and about 17 more names in Klapoetke's Encyclopedia, against about 150 verified entities needed; Keshavarz and Sadeghi's experimental column is the NIST WebBook. The eleven primaries behind NIST's nitramine rows were read: five of the eight eligible solid nitramines now rest on a primary page, and NIST's rows turn out to be re-evaluations that differ from the papers' printed figures by up to 15 kJ/mol. A 2009 recommended set (Miroshnichenko et al.) adds six measured solid nitramines and shows methyldinitramine is a liquid, so about 10 of about 13 eligible solid nitramines are now verified, still far short of the 150 R2 needs. Pedley 1986 confirms, on readable pages, the Medard-Thomas digits for pentaerythritol, EDNA, DINA, nitroguanidine, trimethylolpropane trinitrate and dipentaerythritol hexanitrate (same lineage, so no independent root). Stegeman's 1945 OSRD report (found on archive.org) is a primary for nitroguanidine, HMX, DINA and tetryl; its HMX value is an outlier. Edwards 1953 and Cundall 1978 (the sublimation primaries for PETN and, new here, RDX, HMX, tetryl and nitroguanidine) were read. Values are quoted as facts with locators. No number of the preregistration changed and no method was scored.
