### Gasteiger Dipole Moment can be compared with this-run QC Debye

- The mixed ordinary-versus-Quantum allowlist has one row: Gasteiger magnitude (`dipole.magnitude`) versus `{provider}.dipole_moment` in Debye. EEM, QEq, and charged species are refused. The Δ is arithmetic and does not require identical geometry: ordinary uses that execution's coordinates; a single-point QC number uses the submitted geometry; an optimization or frequency job uses the optimized geometry.
