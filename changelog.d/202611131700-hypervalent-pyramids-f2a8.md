### Four more hypervalent geometries from the papers: BrF5, IF5, ClF5 and IF7
- **BrF5, IF5 and ClF5 (square pyramids) and IF7 (pentagonal bipyramid) now get a VSEPR starting geometry**, read from the original papers rather than a compilation (Robiette, Bradley and Brier 1971; Bodenseh, Huttner and Nowicki 1976; Adams, Thompson and Bartell 1970). The registry (`docs/sources.toml`) and the literature log record what each paper does and does not determine, including the negative results.
- **ClF5's two bond lengths are an assumption, and the row says so**: the microwave paper fixes only the apical-basal angle (86.5 +/- 1.0 degrees) and states that a complete structure is not possible, with both lengths about 1.65 A. NIST CCCBDB's single 1.650 A and its D3h point group (which cannot describe a square pyramid) were not usable as listed. A real ORCA B3LYP/def2-SVP optimisation from that start gives apical 1.664, basal 1.709 A and 85.6 degrees (`tests/test_hypervalent_orca.py`).
- BrF5's basal-basal angle, 89.53 degrees as NIST lists it independently, is reproduced by the builder from the 84.8 degree apical-basal angle. IF3 and XeF6 stay unsupported (no gas-phase structure found; XeF6 is not one geometry).
