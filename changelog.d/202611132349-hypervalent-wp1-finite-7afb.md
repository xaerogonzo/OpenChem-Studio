### No NaN or infinity is published as a result: PF5, xenon and iodine(III) no longer show Gasteiger charges that were NaN
- **A calculator, descriptor or per-atom dataset whose number is NaN or infinite is now refused as the method's limit (`ELEMENT_OUTSIDE_PARAMETER_SET`)**, naming the elements where it can, instead of being shown. Measured before: Gasteiger charges were NaN for PF5, PCl5, IF5, XeF2, XeF4, phenyl-iodine diacetate and CH3-PF4, in Properties and in the per-atom data.
- **A per-atom set with one bad atom is refused whole** (charges with a hole do not sum to the structure's charge); the other descriptors of the same molecule are unaffected, and sulfate, DMSO, perchlorate, phosphate, ethanol, benzene, SF6 and SF4 publish what they did. The guard is `domain/finite_guard.py`, applied in `DescriptorService`.
