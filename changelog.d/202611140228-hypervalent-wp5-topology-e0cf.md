### Topology analysis now runs on a drawn ClF3, BrF3 or ClF5
- **Topology analysis, atom eccentricity and atom distance degree are allowed on a held hypervalent structure.** They are arithmetic on the heavy-atom graph, and were refused only because topology analysis counted a chiral centre on five- and six-coordinate atoms (fixed in the stereocentre work). Checked against a breadth-first search and the textbook definition of each index (Wiener, hyper-Wiener, Harary, Platt, Randic, Balaban with bond-order distances, Szeged, Wiener polarity, cyclomatic number) on eight held structures, including ones with a ring, an aromatic ring and a stereocentre.
- **Still refused on a held structure, measured wrong or meaningless:** geometry analysis, surface analysis, the dipole moment (0.00 for a T-shaped ClF3), polarizability, logD, solubility, the CNS score and the regulatory screen.
