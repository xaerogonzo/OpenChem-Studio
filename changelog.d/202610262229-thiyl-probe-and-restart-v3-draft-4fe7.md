### Thiyl probe, its calibration, a halogen reference set and a draft restart-v3 preregistration (benchmarks only; no calculator changed)
- `benchmarks/thiyl_probe/` shows that the imaginary modes on the methyl- and ethyl-thiyl radicals are spurious (an ill-conditioned analytic M06-2X Hessian; a numerical Hessian is clean) and that a radical which passes can still carry a quiet analytic error of about 0.3 kcal/mol, so "an imaginary mode appeared" cannot be the rule for when to compute numerical frequencies. `calibrate.py` measures how the smallest stability eigenvalue relates to that shift on radicals the two earlier benchmarks had already computed (nothing in it is held out).
- `benchmarks/bde_heldout/candidates_halogen.json` holds six chlorine- and fluorine-containing C-H bonds from the saved ATcT table, chosen from the table's names alone; `tests/test_bde_heldout_halogen.py` recomputes every one.
- `benchmarks/restart_v3/PREREGISTRATION.md` is a DRAFT, not frozen: nothing in it has been run. It waits on four decisions (D1 to D4).
