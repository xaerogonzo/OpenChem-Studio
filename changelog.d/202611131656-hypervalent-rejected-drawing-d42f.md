### A drawing the molecule cannot hold is now reported, not silently dropped
- **Drawing chlorine trifluoride left the previous molecule (chlorine monofluoride) in the 3D viewer and in every result.** RDKit allows chlorine only one bond, so it refuses a drawn ClF3 (also BrF3 and ClF5), and the editor discarded the refusal with a bare `return`: the canvas showed the new drawing while the molecule kept the old structure. The window now says so in the status bar and the console, in RDKit's own words (the atom and its valence), and leaves the message up until a drawing is applied again. The molecule, its identity, conformers, results and undo/redo history are untouched.
- This does not yet make ClF3 representable: the structure check already accepts these hypervalent structures while the parser does not, and the 3D builder is not VSEPR-aware (XeF2 comes out bent, XeF4 and SF6 with junk angles). Accepting them and giving them a VSEPR starting geometry is planned as separate steps.
