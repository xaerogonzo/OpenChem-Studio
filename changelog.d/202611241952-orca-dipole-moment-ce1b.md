### A quantum run now reports the dipole moment ORCA computed
- **ORCA runs (sp, opt, opt_freq, NMR, ...) publish `orca.dipole_moment`**, read from ORCA's own "Total Dipole Moment" block (the last one, the final geometry), with the vector in Debye and whether the value is origin-independent (net charge zero) in its provenance. The registry `dipole_moment` cannot do this for ClF3, BrF3 or ClF5 (its Gasteiger charges are NaN there); a quantum run needs no charge model. Measured on real ORCA 6.1.1, ClF3 B3LYP/def2-SVP: 0.984 D, along the molecule's C2 axis. Compound jobs (delta-SCF, Fukui, LED) get none, because their output holds several species.
