### The engine can hold a hypervalent structure (ClF3, BrF3, ClF5) when asked; nothing asks yet
- **A structure OpenChem's hypervalent table accepts but RDKit will not sanitise now raises `UnsanitizableStructureError`**, a subclass of `InvalidStructureError`, so every existing caller behaves exactly as before. Its message says the structure is accepted but not supported yet, and names the atoms.
- **`ChemistryEngine.hypervalent_mol_from_molblock` and `set_structure_from_molblock(..., allow_hypervalent=True)` are the opt-in path.** The valence test is waived only on the accepted atoms; any other wrong atom, or a ligand the table does not list, is still refused. The identity (SMILES, InChI) must read back from the stored molblock or the structure is refused. The shared rule is `checkers.valence.is_allowed_hypervalent`, used by the structure check too.
- The window, the undo commands, the conformer service and the charge default pass the flag (see the calculator-refusal entry), after the calculators were made to refuse what they cannot compute.
