### Results keeps every method and Quantum run

- Ordinary calculator clicks are stored as property executions (method/model and parameters, a new id on every Run) beside the existing cache, not by widening the cache key. Results lists those executions and Quantum `run_id`s, groups methods so they collapse, labels rows whose structure no longer matches as historical, and offers delete and compare that refuse incompatible pairs. Goal-wizard Quantum staging reads the wizard's own Run-on box.
