### Design note: hypervalent structures that RDKit accepts, and a defect scan behind it
- **`docs/HYPERVALENT_COVERAGE_DESIGN.md`** records what was measured on SF6, PF5, XeF4, XeF2, SF4, IF5, PCl5, Ph-SF5, an iodine(III) reagent and six ordinary controls, why a class-wide refusal is the wrong design (RDKit's acceptance is arbitrary; the same atoms are everyday sulfone, phosphate and perchlorate chemistry), and seven work packages with acceptance tests. The two calculators and one dataset that showed objective defects are the first targets: the always-on Gasteiger charges are NaN for P(V), Xe and I(III) centres, and topology analysis reports a chiral centre for any five- or six-coordinate atom.
- `benchmarks/hypervalent/defect_scan.py` reproduces the numbers (non-finite values, exceptions, spurious stereocentres only; nothing that needs a reference). No application behaviour changes.
