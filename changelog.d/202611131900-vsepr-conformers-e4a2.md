### Hypervalent molecules get a VSEPR geometry in conformer generation, labelled as unoptimised
- **XeF2, XeF4, SF4, SF6, PF5 (and ClF3 / BrF3 when a structure can be held) are built from the VSEPR template instead of embedded.** Embedding gave XeF2 bent at 112 degrees and SF6 with 42 degree angles. The geometry is one deterministic conformer with an unknown energy; `converged` still means "reached a minimum" and is not raised by it, and the new `unoptimised` count says what it is.
- **It is labelled honestly.** Its method is `vsepr_template` (not `rdkit+MMFF94/UFF`), the provenance records `force_field: null`, `geometry_method` and `conformers_unoptimised`, the status line says "idealised ... VSEPR geometry ..., not optimised", and a conformer chooser reads "idealised VSEPR geometry, not optimised". ORCA optimize is what refines it.
- **A hypervalent structure with no recorded measured geometry (IF3, IF5, mixed ligands, PCl5) now fails with a stated reason instead of returning a wrong shape.** Ordinary molecules, sulfate and perchlorate included, take the unchanged search.
- ClF3 and BrF3 reach this path now that the window holds them (see the calculator-refusal entry).
