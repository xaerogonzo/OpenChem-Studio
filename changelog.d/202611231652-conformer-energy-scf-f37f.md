### Compare SCF energy across conformer geometries of the same structure

- Quantum history compare can tabulate SCF energy in Hartree for two jobs that share molecular identity, method, basis, charge, multiplicity, and solvent even when the submitted geometries differ. The Δ is an electronic-energy difference, not a free-energy difference. Enthalpy and Gibbs still need matching geometry. A rewrite of the same molecule UUID is not treated as the same structure.
