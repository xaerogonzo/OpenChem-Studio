### Restart v3: a computed 0 K to 298 K step for the propanethiol references, and the runner for Arms C, D and C-Br (benchmarks only; no calculator changed)
- `benchmarks/restart_v3/thermal_step.py` replaces the borrowed +5.2 kJ/mol with a step computed from B3LYP/def2-TZVPD frequencies. The method gives 5.16 kJ/mol for ethanethiol against Ervin 2015's published 5.2, and 5.09 and 4.98 for 1- and 2-propanethiol; the two references move by -0.02 and -0.05 kcal/mol (87.72 +- 0.62 and 87.08 +- 0.63). The records are committed and `tests/test_restart_v3_references.py` recomputes the steps from them.
- A caution recorded in the preregistration: two of three optimised 2-propanethiyl structures are saddle points (-233 cm-1) lying 2.3 kcal/mol below the minimum, which the step rejects as it rejects any non-minimum; it bears on how Arm D's isopropyl bond may behave.
- `run_restart_v3.py` and `restart_v3_core.py` add the numerical-frequency stage (one `NumFreq` job for a radical still imaginary after the re-run, no displaced restart, enthalpy kept and labelled when the numerical mode is below -20 cm-1, none above 12 atoms) and the bond lists, references and reading rules of the three arms. Tested with excerpts of real ORCA output and a stand-in ORCA; nothing has been run on ORCA through it.
