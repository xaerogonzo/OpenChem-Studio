### Results history is honest about failures, always-on rows, and compare

- Failed ordinary calculator runs are kept as property executions even when the cache already holds the same slot. Always-on descriptors stay current-value rows. Deleting a Quantum job withdraws the cache numbers it projected (`source_run_id`, not a wider cache key). Compatible non-NMR history compare is a named-value table; NMR stays per-atom. Opening an older file does not invent execution ids.
