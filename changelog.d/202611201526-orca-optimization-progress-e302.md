### A running ORCA geometry optimization now shows its progress, and the ORCA Log is ORCA's real output
- The Quantum Chemistry panel shows the cycle, energy, energy change and the convergence criteria still unmet while an ORCA optimization runs, and says how often ORCA rebuilt its optimizer.
- The ORCA Log used to be the last line of each output chunk, a random sample that never showed cycle headers or convergence tables; it is now the complete output. Status text (elapsed time, progress) moved to a line above the log, and a failure's reason is still written to the log.
- Fixed a latent crash-in-waiting in the menu bar: Theme and the Stereo Group Labels, Electron Display and Atom Numbers submenus were held only by a local variable, so a garbage collection at the wrong moment removed them and their entries. They are now held on the window.
- New button Stop and keep geometry: it stops a running ORCA optimization like Cancel but keeps the geometry it had reached as a conformer labelled ORCA partial geometry, NOT converged (with its cycle). It is never treated as a finished optimization, has no energy, and is not the geometry later calculations prefer. If no sound geometry can be read, nothing is kept.
- The progress readout adds a slow-progress note (tiny energy change over 10 cycles, a criterion still unmet, ORCA rebuilding its optimizer in at least half the cycles) and the ORCA Log keeps at most 100,000 lines.
