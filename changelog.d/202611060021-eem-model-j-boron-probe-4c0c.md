### EEM: Model J measured against CHELPG and stays paused; a boron probe says not yet
- Jiroušková's Merz-Kollman EEM model (Model J) was measured against ORCA's CHELPG charges on 37 molecules, with the shipped default EEM as the yardstick, under rules registered first (`benchmarks/charges/models/eem_second_models_preregistration.md` sections 13 and 15). Mean error 0.134 e and 1.13 times the yardstick's, but one heavy atom is 0.68 e off (the limit was 0.5 e), so by the registered rule it stays paused and is not offered. Eleven atoms are over 0.4 e and the default EEM is within 0.14 e of Model J on every one, so this is the EEM family disagreeing with CHELPG, not a fault particular to J; a proper Merz-Kollman reference is what would settle it.
- A probe of whether one fitted boron type could be added (16 boron compounds, leave-one-out against CHELPG) came to 0.19 e mean error but 0.65 e on the worst boron atom (a hydridic B-H boron against halide and oxygen boron), over the registered 0.5 e. Boron stays refused by both models; nothing ships from the probe.
