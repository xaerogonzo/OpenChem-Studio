### Results stay put, and a second ORCA job is refused

- Renaming a molecule, or moving it on the canvas without changing the chemistry, no longer clears calculator results or marks them stale. A repeated copy of a result that is already saved no longer marks the project unsaved.
- Compare fills its columns from saved results, and Open in Calculator Inspector opens the molecule whose pane you clicked.
- pH charts use their own names. Results can expand or collapse all sections. New Molecule is on the Project Explorer menu.
- Run recommended for Identity, Charge, and Shape includes the isotope envelope, oxidation states, intramolecular contacts, and the dihedral table.
- Only one ORCA job runs at a time, and the status bar names it. xtb can be downloaded from its stable Windows release. Psi4's Get button opens the v1.11 install page.
