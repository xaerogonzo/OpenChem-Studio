### ORCA runs on a drawn ClF3 (and BrF3): the VSEPR start is optimized and kept
- **An ORCA optimization or single point now starts from a held hypervalent geometry.** ORCA needs atoms, charge and coordinates, so the run path reads the stored conformer through the opt-in parse (descriptors still refuse the structure). The panel's run display, the source-atom map and the charge default use it too, and the Tautomers button says the structure is outside its scope instead of raising.
- **Measured on a real ORCA 6.1.1, B3LYP/def2-SVP, from the VSEPR geometry:** F-Cl-F 87.2, 87.2 and 174.3 degrees, Cl-F 1.654 (equatorial) and 1.740 Angstrom (axial). The T-shape holds; the lengths are longer than the gas-phase 1.597 and 1.697 A, as a small basis gives. `tests/test_hypervalent_orca.py` checks the written input (atom order, charge, multiplicity, Angstrom, no hydrogen) without ORCA and runs the optimization when `OPENCHEM_ORCA_EXE` names one.
