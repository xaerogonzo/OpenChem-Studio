### 58 template drawings no longer overlap or cross
- The Template Library drew many bridged and polycyclic molecules (tropane and morphinan alkaloids, adamantanes, camphor and pinene terpenoids, cages, quinuclidine and similar) with atoms on top of each other or crossing bonds. `tools/curate_template_layouts.py` now picks a better drawing (a crossing-free planar layout of the ring block, CoordGen, or a projected 3D view), checks it is the same molecule by InChIKey with stereo, and stores it as the entry's `molblock`; the original SMILES are pinned in `tests/fixtures/curated_template_smiles.json`. Templates flagged only for a long, narrow chain are unchanged. The layout screen's stretched-bond limit is now 3 (was 2), since flat cage drawings stretch their outer face.
