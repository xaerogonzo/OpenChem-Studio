### AsF5, SeF4, PCl5 and the anions ClF4- and XeF5- get a VSEPR geometry; a drawn XeF5- is no longer rejected
- **Five more starting geometries from the papers** (Clippard and Bartell 1970 for AsF5, Bowater, Brown and Burden 1968 for SeF4, Scheibe et al. 2020 for ClF4-, Christe et al. 1991 for XeF5-; PCl5 from NIST CCCBDB). The builder now accepts a formal charge on the centre (the lone pairs follow the outer shell less the charge), has a pentagonal planar template for XeF5-, and keys each reference by charge. The two anions are CRYSTAL structures and say so.
- **A drawn XeF5- was rejected as invalid.** RDKit refuses it, but the acceptance test compared the atom's effective valence with RDKit's list and xenon's list already holds six. The test now asks whether RDKit actually refuses the atom and the hypervalent table allows it (and leaves anything RDKit sanitises, perchlorate included, alone). Arsenic and selenium join the table, since a paper was read for each.
- Real ORCA B3LYP/def2-SVP (def2-SVPD, charge -1 for the anions) keeps every new shape: SeF4 100.0 and 170.2 degrees against 100.55 and 169.20 measured; ClF4- stays square planar and XeF5- a regular planar pentagon (`tests/test_hypervalent_orca.py`). XeOF4, SOF4 and SF5Cl are read or located and not shipped: they need per-ligand lengths and a Xe=O or S=O ligand.
