### Properties works on a drawn ClF3: five calculators verified against independent oracles
- **Elemental analysis, mass spectrum, substance analysis, oxidation states and per-atom accessible surface now run on a structure RDKit cannot sanitise (ClF3, BrF3, ClF5)**; the Properties header reads "ClF3 Molecule" again instead of the molecule's name alone. Every other calculator still refuses with `HYPERVALENT_UNSUPPORTED`. The list is `calculation_input.HYPERVALENT_VERIFIED_CALCULATORS`.
- **Each one was checked against values worked out separately, on ClF3, BrF3, SF6 and PF5** (`tests/test_hypervalent_calculators.py`): percent composition from IUPAC atomic weights, the chlorine (3:1), bromine (1:1) and sulfur isotope patterns from natural abundances, oxidation numbers by hand, and per-atom surface area against a second Shrake-Rupley implementation written in the test.
- **Measured and deliberately NOT allowed:** topology analysis reports one chiral centre for octahedral SF6, geometry analysis includes a Dreiding energy for ClF3 that means nothing, surface analysis splits polar and hydrophobic area on charges these molecules do not have, the additive polarizability of SF6 is 21% below experiment, and the regulatory screen said "no matches" on a structure it could not read. **SF6, PF5, XeF4 and SF4 are accepted by RDKit, so they never met the refusal and still show those same defects on the ordinary path** (a follow-up).
