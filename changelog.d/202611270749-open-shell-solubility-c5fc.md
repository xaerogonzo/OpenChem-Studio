### Solubility now warns that ESOL runs low on a radical
- **A structure with unpaired electrons gets a 'Baseline reliability' row first in the Solubility report, the same note in its limitations and provenance, and no ICH M9 screen.** Measured against the five stable nitroxides with a published pure-water solubility (4-substituted TEMPO, Zhou et al. 2020), ESOL came out too LOW on every one, by 0.6 to 2.6 log units (mean 1.7), more than its usual error on three of five. Whether the error belongs to the unpaired electron is not established (no closed-shell comparison was measured, and polar, very soluble compounds are where ESOL underpredicts anyway). The AqSolDB baseline, which nobody checked on a radical, gets a 'not checked' note. A warning, not a refusal: the number is still given.
