### Charge models no longer give a radical charges they were not measured to deserve
- **Gasteiger, EEM (Bultinck) and both Ionescu EEM methods now return no charges for a structure with unpaired electrons; QEq still does and says so.** Measured against ORCA Hirshfeld charges on nine radicals and their closed-shell parents (limit 0.05 e of extra error, fixed before the run): QEq +0.035 e passed; Gasteiger +0.073, EEM +0.084, Ionescu 6-31G* +0.062 and 6-31G** +0.127 failed. The refusal reaches the dipole, the ESP pane, the Gasteiger per-atom charges and pH charges, orbital electronegativity and the ASA+/ASA- split (the geometry-only surface areas stay). Triplet O2 drawn `O=O` and S2 are now recognised as open-shell by the NEEMP and Jiroukova refusals too.
