### A VSEPR starting-geometry builder for hypervalent molecules (not wired in yet)
- **`chem/vsepr_geometry.py` builds coordinates for ClF3 and BrF3 (T-shaped), XeF2 (linear), XeF4 (square planar), SF4 (seesaw), SF6 (octahedral) and PF5 (trigonal bipyramidal)** from the molecule's graph (one centre, terminal single bonds, no charge; lone pairs from the electron count) and published bond lengths and angles, each read from NIST's CCCBDB experimental-geometry table and recorded with its original source. The result is an idealised starting geometry: no energy, not optimised.
- It reports three outcomes apart: built, hypervalent but unsupported (no measured geometry recorded, mixed ligands, no template; the caller must not fall back to embedding, which gives a wrong shape), and not applicable (everything else, left to the normal conformer path). Nothing is estimated from radii. ClF5, IF3, IF5, IF7 and XeF6 are deliberately not covered yet.
- Nothing calls it yet; the conformer pipeline uses it in the next step.
