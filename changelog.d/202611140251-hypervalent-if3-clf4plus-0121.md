### IF3, ClF4+, IF4-, BrF4-, IF2- and AsF4- get their measured 3D geometry
- **A drawn IF3 is built as the planar T of the crystal** (I-F 198.3 and 187.2 pm, F-I-F 160.3 and 80.2 degrees) instead of the trigonal planar shape RDKit embeds, and **ClF4+ as the seesaw of its SbF6- salt** (Cl-F 1.617 and 1.530 A, 173.9 and 103.1 degrees), the first cation. Both are crystals and say so; the bridging contacts to neighbours are omitted. Real ORCA B3LYP/def2-SVPD keeps both shapes.
- **IF4-, BrF4-, IF2- and AsF4- are built from their crystal structures too** (IF4- I-F 200.7 pm square planar, BrF4- Br-F 1.89 A square planar, IF2- 207.9 pm linear, AsF4- a seesaw). IF5(2-) stays unsupported: its only lengths are calculated.
- **A trigonal pyramid template** (three bonds and a lone pair) was added with **XeO3** (crystal mean Xe-O 1.76 A, 103 degrees) and **BrO2F** (one averaged Br-(O,F) distance of 167.4 pm for all three ligands, because the crystal's oxygen and fluorine are disordered; the row says so).
- **SbCl5** (gas electron diffraction, axial 2.338 and equatorial 2.277 A) and **PCl2F3** (chlorines equatorial, P-F 1.593 and 1.546, P-Cl 2.004 A) are built too; antimony joins the hypervalent rule table. **XeF6** is built as a labelled MODEL (a C3v distorted octahedron, Bartell and Gavin's Model C: three Xe-F 1.912 and three 1.862 A): the molecule has no single measured geometry, and a quantum optimisation at the B3LYP/def2-SVP level relaxes it to a regular octahedron, so it is a starting point only. **BrO2F** now has separate Br=O (1.60) and Br-F (1.80 A) lengths and its O-Br-O (107.4) and O-Br-F (100) angles from the 2019 adduct crystal (Supporting Information, Table S4).
- **Every geometry now says how well its numbers are known** (`measured`, `model`, `calculated` or `estimated`) in the conformer's note, and three more species are built: **TeF7-** (crystal, with the paper's ambiguous table said), **IF5(2-)** (a published calculation, labelled calculated) and **PCl3F2** (lengths transferred from the measured PCl2F3, labelled estimated). XeF6 is marked a model and ClF5 estimated.
- **A hypervalent centre with organic or hydrogen groups now gets its measured core with the groups grown on it** (a new `chem/vsepr_graft.py`): triphenyldifluorophosphorane (two crystals), (CH3)2PF3 and CH3PF4 (gas electron diffraction) and PH3F2 (rotation-vibration spectra) are built with every hydrogen, several conformers and the core exactly as measured; another carbon ligand than the measured kind is labelled an estimate. Aryl-SF5 and iodine(III) reagents keep the ordinary embedding until their cores are added.
