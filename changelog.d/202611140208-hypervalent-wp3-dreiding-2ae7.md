### Geometry no longer shows a DREIDING energy for SF6, PF5, SF4, IF5, perchlorate and similar over-coordinated centres
- **DREIDING refuses to type a centre that has more neighbours than its type allows**, so the Geometry report leaves the DREIDING energy empty instead of showing a number that means nothing. Measured before: SF6 typed `S_3` gave 708 kcal/mol, PF5 163, SF4 394, IF5 as a one-bond halogen 1053, a perchlorate chlorine 404, phenyl-SF5 623 and phenyl-iodine diacetate 256.
- **The limit comes from the atom's own electrons for sp3 centres** (neutral C 4, N 3, P 3, O and S 2; N+, P+ 4, S+ 3, B- 4), one bond for a monovalent halogen, three for sp2 and resonant centres. Ammonium, phosphonium, sulfonium, borate, silane, thiols, amides, benzene, iodobenzene and PPh3 keep their types. The MMFF94 and UFF energies are unchanged.
