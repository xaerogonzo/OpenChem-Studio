OpenChem Studio - privacy

OpenChem Studio collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no telemetry, no usage
statistics, no crash reporting, and it does not check for updates to itself. Your
structures, projects and settings stay on your computer.

The program contacts other services only when you use a feature that needs one, and only
the service that feature names. Nothing is sent in the background:

  - PubChem and ChEMBL, when you look up or search compounds
  - RCSB, when you fetch a protein structure by its PDB id
  - IBM RXN, when you use reaction prediction, with your own API key
  - Anthropic, OpenAI, or a local Ollama, when you use the AI assistant, with your own
    API key
  - Adoptium, GitHub, SourceForge, PyTorch and PyG, when you ask the program to set up
    Java, AutoDock Vina, the NMR database or the pKa predictor

You can turn any plugin off from the Plugins menu. The AI assistant and reaction
prediction do nothing until you give them a key.

The full policy, including what is sent to each service and how releases are signed, is in
docs\CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md in the installed folder and at
https://github.com/xaerogonzo/OpenChem-Studio/blob/master/docs/CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md

Releases are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "unknown publisher":
choose More info, then Run anyway.
