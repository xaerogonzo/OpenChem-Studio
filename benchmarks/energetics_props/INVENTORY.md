# Energetic-materials evidence inventory (Phase B)

Generated by `benchmarks/energetics_props/inventory_report.py` from `inventory.json` (counted 2026-10-25); every number below is read from that file, none is typed. The design is in `PREREGISTRATION.md`. **No corpus has been built and no method scored.** This inventory holds counts and locators: it was a counting stage, not a verdict on the sources. The values themselves are read from the held books and papers and quoted as facts with their pages (see `PETN_EVIDENCE.md` for the first such pass); a bulk corpus stays beside the library.

## 1. How to read the counts

* A count is **entries or table rows that print a numeric value for a property, before any structure identification, value-kind classification, temperature or form check, or verification** (preregistration sections 3, 7 and 8). Every count is therefore an UPPER bound on usable distinct compounds, and a sum over sources is an upper bound with heavy double counting.
* Counts were made by script over the PDF or EPUB text layer, or read off a named table by hand; the method is recorded beside each, with the hand-check error rate where one was measured. Where a figure is a range the range is stated.
* Nothing here says a value is **right**. Several held books copy values from one another, and the printed errors in section 4 were found only by comparing sources or reading the rendered page.
* A book that prints an enthalpy of formation without saying whether it is measured or calculated is `unlabelled` (preregistration 3.1) and is not a reference by itself.

## 7. Upper bounds on usable entities, and what they allow

Each row adds the counts of section 3 that could in principle feed the property, scaling the uncovered Klapoetke entries at the covered rate. The sum double-counts every compound two books both print, which is most of the well-known ones, and none of it has passed identity, value-kind, temperature or form checks: it is a ceiling on entries, not an estimate of distinct compounds. The preregistration asks (11.2) whether 300 VERIFIED eligible entities exist per property; that is measurable only after identity resolution in the corpus build.
