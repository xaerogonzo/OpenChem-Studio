## Known faults in the handbooks themselves

The parser reads what the page prints, and the page is sometimes wrong. Checked on the rendered pages: Lange prints 2,2,3,3-tetramethylbutane's melting point as -100.7 (it melts at +100.7), triethylborane's as -02.9 (-92.9 with a digit lost), 1,5-pentanediamine's as -129.7 (the value of pentane, one row above) and 3-bromopyridine's as 142-143 (it melts at -27); 4-bromobenzophenone's row has its values one column to the right; the CRC prints 6-methyl-5-hepten-2-one's boiling point under melting point. A within-row check (a melting point at or above the normal boiling point of the same row) and a malformed-number check (02.9) refuse some of these; the rest are what the label-quality subsets above exist to measure. The corpus measures handbook values, and says so.
