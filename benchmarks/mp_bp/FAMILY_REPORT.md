## What this says and does not say

- The pooled comparison with `RESULT.md` is the measure of how much of Candidate C's headline accuracy depends on the family having been seen: the headline pools entities of every family, this leaves a family out.
- Marrero-Gani and Joback are reported on the same entities for scale. They were not fitted here, so their numbers do not change with the family left out; where one family is far harder for them than for Candidate C, that is a statement about the family, not about generalisation.
- Families overlap in chemistry (a nitro compound with a halogen is `nitro` here because the first match wins), so a held-out family is not chemically isolated from the others; this is the preregistered family definition, not a clean scaffold split.
- Nothing here promotes a calculator or changes a verdict.
