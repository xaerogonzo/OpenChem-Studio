## What happened in the run

* 21 structures (10 parents, 10 radicals, the hydrogen atom), 60 records, all chains closed, **no job failed, no structure is missing**. The sum of recorded job times is 14.3 h.
* **This is the first run in which rule R1 fired.** Arm C, D-Ph and C-Br never did. **Every thiyl radical conformer the first run's two stages left with a mode below -20 cm-1 got the one numerical job**: the analytic Hessians gave -296 to -2,094 cm-1 (the spurious imaginary mode of the thiyl probe), and the `Calc_Hess` re-run did not remove any of them. Nine numerical jobs ran: ethanethiol 2, 1-propanethiol 2 (conformers 0 and 1), 2-propanethiol 3, tert-butanethiol 1, methanethiol 1.
* **Eight of the nine came back with no mode below -20 cm-1** (the lowest vibrations of the ones that supply a bond are 230, 203 and 601 cm-1 in `report.txt`). **One did not**: the 2-propanethiol radical's conformer 1 stayed at **-201.7 cm-1** after the numerical job. By the rule (decision D2) its enthalpy is kept and labelled "frequencies not verified", with no displaced restart; it is the conformer that supplies the 2-propanethiol bond (its enthalpy is the lowest of the three), so **that bond's value (-1.41) carries the label.** The label sits on the best conformer and not on a marginal one: the other two conformers' numerical jobs were clean and 0.54 and 0.61 kcal/mol higher in enthalpy (the three analytic enthalpies had agreed within 0.005 kcal/mol). **Its numerical shift (+0.49) is about half of the other two's (+1.03 and +1.10)**; had the bond come from a conformer that behaved like those, it would be about 0.5 kcal/mol higher. That is arithmetic on the records, not a correction, and none is applied.
* **The numerical enthalpy was above the analytic one in every case, by +0.49 to +1.39 kcal/mol** (ethanethiol +0.85, 2-propanethiol +0.49, tert-butanethiol +1.39, methanethiol +0.99): the direction that moves each of these bonds toward its reference, and the size the thiyl probe had measured (about 1 kcal/mol).
* **tert-butanethiol's radical has 14 atoms**, the largest in the benchmark and exactly `VALIDATED_ATOMS`, so its record carries no size note. Its numerical job was the longest of the arm (79 minutes); its first run's two stages took 44 and 38 minutes. Numerical jobs took 3 minutes (methanethiol, 5 atoms), 14 (ethanethiol, 8 atoms), 41 to 59 (the propyl radicals, 11 atoms) and 79 (14 atoms): 6.3 hours in total of the 14.3.
* Nothing was re-run, selected or excluded after seeing the numbers.

## What it shows, and what it does not

* **All four deciding errors are negative (-0.95 to -2.90), as in Arm C, Arm C-Br and the first two arms** (Arm D-Ph's single value was mixed against its determinations): the calculation runs low here too. The mean (-1.78) is more negative than Arm C's (-0.94) and in the range of the first run's (-1.53, 22 bonds) and the held-out arm's (-1.25, 27 bonds); with four correlated references, a difference of that size is not a finding, and no correction is applied. The anchor sensitivity above can only make it more negative.
* **The largest error is 1-propanethiol, and it is the only one of the four whose radical value is analytic.** Its conformers 0 and 1 had imaginary modes and got numerical jobs; conformer 2 had none, was not sent to one, and its analytic enthalpy is 0.55 and 0.65 kcal/mol *below* the two numerical ones, so it is the conformer the bond uses. The three bonds with numerical radicals have errors of -1.85, -1.41 and -0.95; the one with an analytic radical, -2.90. This is the "quiet error" the thiyl probe and decision D2(a) stated as R1's known limit (R1 misses a radical with no imaginary mode whose analytic enthalpy is still off by a few tenths of a kcal/mol): **it is one bond, so it is an observation and not a result**, and whether conformer 2's value is such an error was not measured here. **A numerical job on that conformer alone was run afterwards and is reported below, beside this arm and outside its verdict.**
* **Per section 9, a PARTIAL permits** the calculator's S-H value for a thiol to be shown, with the statement "checked against four held-out S-H references, of about 0.6 kcal/mol, that share an anchor" and Arm D's error beside the other arms', **as a separate change** that adopts the numerical step in `chem/bond_dissociation.py` first and has its own driven run on real ORCA. **That change has not been made and is not part of this result**; the calculator still gives S-H no value. Note what the statement must also carry: the calculation runs 1.8 kcal/mol low on these four, a PARTIAL and not a PASS, and one of the four values carries "frequencies not verified".
* It is a statement about **these four alkyl thiols** (the 14-atom tert-butyl is the largest), with references that are three derived and one published and that share an anchor. It says nothing about thiophenol and the aromatic thiols (Arm D-Ph, reported only), about disulfides or thioethers, or about a thiol with other functional groups.

Most of the -2.90 is not an analytic-versus-numerical artefact: of the 1.50 kcal/mol by which this bond's error sits below the mean of the arm's other three (-1.40), the follow-up accounts for 0.34, about a fifth. * **Consequence for the calculator question:** adopting R1 as frozen would leave about a third of a kcal/mol of low bias (0.34 here) on an analytic radical of this kind. Whether to extend the numerical job to radicals with no imaginary mode (it would cost an hour here) is a decision for a separate change and, if it changes the rule, an amendment with its own held-out check; it is not made here.
